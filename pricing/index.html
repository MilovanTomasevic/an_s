Pricing by controlled operating scope

Pay for the execution and evidence responsibility you place under control—not for seats alone.

Start with the systems, critical processes, top-level executions, environments, retention and operating responsibility in scope. User access remains a security and administration concern; it is not the primary measure of advanexus value.

The value unit

Price the control boundary, not the number of people who can see it.

The annual software scope follows the supported systems and business processes under control, their top-level execution volume, evidence and retention obligations, environments and accepted operating responsibility. Implementation, deployment and enhanced support remain visible.

    What the buyer is scoping

    03

    Know what each value unit puts under control.

    These units make a proposal reviewable. They are contractual planning dimensions, not a fabricated universal runtime billing ledger.

    01

    Tenant

    One isolated organisational control and data boundary. Additional tenants require written architecture and price scope.

    02

    Registered system connection

    One configured connection to a source or target system. A business system can require more than one configuration; capability remains connector-specific.

    03

    Controlled business process or flow

    One agreed end-to-end path with a business outcome, owners, controls, failure path and evidence obligation.

    04

    Top-level controlled execution

    One root run of an accepted process. Retries and child attempts are not presented as separate value units in the public estimate.

    05

    Production or non-production environment

    A licensed operating scope with its own topology and acceptance boundary; managed infrastructure responsibility is priced separately.

    06

    Evidence and retention profile

    The agreed review depth and lifecycle for named object classes. It does not imply universal WORM, legal hold, signing or regulatory retention.

    Complete cost view

    04

    See what recurs, what happens once and what remains variable.

    The approved Order Form, implementation statement and accepted support or SLA schedule remain the commercial authority.

    01

    Annual software

    Responsibility profile, approved system/process/execution scope and selected software add-ons, after any eligible initial-term discount.

    02

    Deployment

    Customer-managed at no platform surcharge, or a visible managed/dedicated planning allowance subject to topology acceptance.

    03

    Support

    Standard business-hours support is included; enhanced support is a percentage of net annual software and requires an executed schedule.

    04

    Implementation

    One-time discovery, configuration, integration, validation, recovery planning, enablement and operational handover. Migration Assurance is scoped here when required.

    Premium and Mission Critical support, on-call coverage, availability targets and service credits apply only when written into an executed schedule after the target environment and operating model are accepted.

    Extended retention is a contracted storage and lifecycle profile for named object classes. It is not an automatic claim of universal WORM, legal hold, KMS signing or regulatory retention.

    Pricing questions

    06

    Read the commercial boundary before you compare totals.

    Are these binding quotes?

    No. They are non-binding public planning estimates. A written proposal and Order Form define the accepted scope, inclusions, exclusions and price.

    Why is implementation separate?

    Production value depends on system integration, permissions, controls, accepted outcomes, evidence coverage, recovery tests and operational handover—not only software access.

    Is advanexus priced per user?

    No. User access is governed and planned, but the public value model follows systems, controlled processes, top-level executions, environments, evidence obligations and accepted responsibility rather than seat count alone.

    Are cloud, model-provider and third-party costs included?

    No. Those costs remain excluded unless an executed proposal explicitly includes a named allowance or pass-through model.

    Do capacity values represent automatic metering?

    No. They are planning and Order Form allowances. The current product does not claim one universal cross-module billing ledger.

    Is Regulated deployment a compliance certification?

    No. It supports a scoped architecture and acceptance programme for regulated operating requirements; it does not certify an organisation or guarantee compliance.

    Does a managed option include an SLA?

    Only an executed SLA for an accepted topology can define availability measurement, exclusions, response objectives and service credits.

    Non-binding planning estimate

    Planning estimate, not an offer or invoice.

    Values exclude VAT and other taxes, travel, provider consumption, cloud usage and unlisted third-party costs. Exchange rates, indexation, topology, data residency, acceptance criteria and legal terms can change the final proposal. Public estimates are reviewed periodically and may change without creating an obligation for either party.
