Logistics and supply chain

Keep operational hand-offs visible from order to delivery outcome.

Orders, inventory, transport, partner APIs and external files create a chain of dependencies where partial loads, late data and ownership gaps can distort SLA or delay reporting.

Discuss a logistics flow →

01

Join heterogeneous operational inputs deliberately.

Capability-aware Sources and Sandbox-managed files can feed controlled SQL, Dataset and Pipeline paths. A failed connector or partial binding remains visible rather than disappearing inside one dashboard status.

02

Turn service levels into explicit checks and metrics.

Data Quality can test parent references and required timestamps; Analytics can compare delay, volume and target metrics under known DatasetVersion and ReportVersion contracts.

03

Investigate the hand-off, not only the final delay.

Execution Story and recorded relations can connect source, transfer, quality, analytics and Case evidence for supported events without claiming a universal supply-chain digital twin.
